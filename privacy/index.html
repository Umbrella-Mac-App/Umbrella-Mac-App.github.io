Privacy Policy

Umbrella is built with a local-first philosophy. We believe your audio streams, app activity, and recordings belong solely to you on your local Mac.

Effective Date: November 2026 • Applies to Umbrella for macOS (umbrella.pixelegg.me)

Summary of Core Principles

Umbrella processes 100% of audio on-device using native macOS Core Audio APIs. We do not operate remote processing servers, do not transmit recorded audio over the internet, and do not track or sell your app usage data.

1. Introduction & Core Principle

This Privacy Policy outlines how Umbrella handles information when you use our macOS desktop application. Umbrella is designed as a standalone system audio utility.

Unlike web-based recorders or cloud services, Umbrella operates completely offline on your physical hardware. We do not collect personal identifiers, track software workflow patterns, or retain logs of your audio activity.

2. Audio Stream Capture & Process Taps

To capture application audio (such as isolating sound from Safari, Chrome, Zoom, Spotify, or Discord), Umbrella requests standard macOS System Audio Recording permissions via native frameworks (such as Core Audio and ScreenCaptureKit).

  • In-Memory Processing: Application audio streams are captured and routed strictly within your Mac's RAM for encoding.
  • Explicit Target Selection: Umbrella only captures audio from applications or input channels you explicitly select in the interface.
  • Zero Cloud Streaming: No audio buffers, fragments, or live feeds are ever streamed or backed up to remote servers.

3. Local Storage & File Ownership

When you record audio, Umbrella saves the resulting file (such as MP3 or AAC formats) directly to your local disk in the destination directory you specify (e.g., your Downloads or Music directory).

You retain total ownership and control over all saved files. Umbrella has no access to your files after they are written, nor do we maintain index catalogs or hidden metadata logs.

4. Payment & License Verification

License sales and order fulfillments are handled by our Merchant of Record, FastSpring. When you purchase a license for Umbrella:

  • FastSpring securely processes payments, billing information, and applicable tax requirements. Umbrella does not store your credit card or financial details.
  • This interaction includes minimal metadata (such as your license key and an anonymized hardware identifier) to prevent license abuse. No audio or usage data is ever included in activation calls.

5. Telemetry & Analytics

We believe essential utilities should remain light and unobtrusive:

Cookies None Used
Telemetry Zero Analytics
Ad Tracking No Third-Parties
